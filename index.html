Six calculators.
One job each.

Herminox is a free, private toolkit for Amazon sellers and buyers. Every calculator runs in your browser — no account, no upload of your numbers. Sellers use FBA Profit for referral + fulfillment + fuel margin, ACoS Breakeven for the PPC spend ceiling before ads erase profit, and Inventory Breakeven for units needed to recover a purchase order in cash (sell − fees). Buyers use True Unit Price to compare packs on a fair per-unit basis, Cost Per Use to weigh sticker price against lifetime uses, and Return & Capital to track return windows and locked cash. Deeper formula write-ups live in the guides and methodology — the homepage stays the map into the tools.

Frequently asked questions

Is this really free, no signup?

Yes. Every calculator runs entirely in your browser. No account, no email, no payment, and calculator inputs are not uploaded.

How accurate is the FBA Profit Calculator?

It uses Amazon’s published referral and FBA fee structure for US. For final pricing decisions, confirm against Seller Central — fees can vary by program and change over time. See Methodology.

What is breakeven ACoS?

The maximum advertising cost of sales percentage you can spend before a PPC sale stops being profitable — driven by your pre-ad margin.

Do I need an Amazon account?

No. Every tool works with numbers you type yourself — nothing connects to your Amazon account.

Seller vs buyer tools?

Seller tools — FBA Profit, ACoS Breakeven, Inventory Breakeven — model margin, ad ceiling, and cash payback. Buyer tools — Unit Price, Cost Per Use, Return & Capital — compare value and return windows.
