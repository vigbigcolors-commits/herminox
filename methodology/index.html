Methodology

How the numbers are built — sources, limits, discipline

Herminox is arithmetic in your browser, coded from Amazon’s published US fee schedules and plain buyer math. This page states what we include, what we omit on purpose, and how we keep guide pages unique enough to deserve an index.

Client-side Published schedules Named gaps Updated August 2026

Product principle

The face of the site is the calculators — not an AI chat and not a blog farm. Guides teach one long-tail decision and end in a tool. Lab Notes explain method and limits in the founder’s voice.

Seller fee model (US)

  • Referral — flat categories and the seven tiered schedules (Apparel, Baby, Furniture, Grocery, Jewelry, Shoes, Watches), including common minimums.
  • FBA fulfillment — size/weight bands from the US rate card effective January 15, 2026.
  • Fuel & logistics — 3.5% of fulfillment fee (toggle on by default; active since April 17, 2026).

Inventory recovery uses sell − fees (cash back toward a PO), not “profit after every dream expense.” See the note on cash vs profit.

What we do not model yet

Monthly/long-term storage, inbound placement fees, return processing fees, and some apparel sub-tiers. Omitting an incomplete row is better than a false sense of precision. Confirm live fees in Seller Central before you commit inventory.

Buyer math

  • Unit price — price ÷ quantity (oz, count, etc.).
  • Cost per use — (price + upkeep) ÷ honest use count.
  • Returns — retailer windows including holiday extensions where documented; locked capital = sum of returnable item values still inside a window.

Privacy

Calculator field values are not uploaded. Aggregate page analytics may run. Details: Privacy Policy and why client-side.

Indexing discipline

One clean URL = one unique formula, table, and worked example. Prefill and tracking query strings are noindex or redirected. We maintain an allowlist and a uniqueness audit; weak pages are strengthened or removed — not multiplied. More: indexing discipline.
